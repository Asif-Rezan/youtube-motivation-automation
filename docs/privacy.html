Privacy Policy

Rezan Video Automation · Updated October 30, 2026

This application is operated by Shahriar Asif Rezan to generate and publish the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel and Facebook Page, Rezan Relaxation. It is a private production tool, not a public sign-in service.

Information we use

With the owner's authorization, the application uses account and Page identifiers, Page names, access tokens, generated videos and metadata, and publication status and schedule information. Facebook permissions are limited to listing authorized Pages, reading their content and engagement as required for publication verification, and managing Page posts. The application does not collect Facebook passwords, private messages, or viewer profiles.

Google authorization is used to upload and verify YouTube videos and manage the application's files in the owner's private Google Drive folder. It does not request Gmail or contacts access. Local models on GitHub Actions generate lyrics, music and images; public model files are downloaded from Hugging Face.

Purpose, storage and sharing

Information is used to identify the correct destination, publish and schedule videos, check processing results, recover failed jobs and avoid duplicate publications. Tokens are stored in encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. Private publication checkpoints and generation history are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Workflow artifacts, including media and checkpoints, are retained for up to seven days. Intended videos and their descriptions become public on the selected YouTube channel and Facebook Page at publication.

Data is processed by Meta, Google, GitHub and model-file hosting providers as needed to run this workflow, under their respective policies. Access tokens are sent only to the API service they authorize. We do not sell personal data or use platform user data for advertising or model training. Google API information is handled according to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Generated source media may be removed after verified publication according to the project's cleanup rules. Publication checkpoints and compact generation history remain until the owner removes them, to prevent duplicates and repeated songs. The owner can stop workflows, revoke platform authorization, delete stored credentials and remove private files. Public posts remain on their platforms until deleted there.

Data deletion requests

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by this application, email asifrezanit@gmail.com with the subject “Rezan Video Automation data deletion”. Include the relevant Page or account ID and describe the request; do not include a password or access token. We will verify authority over the account, delete applicable stored files and credentials, and confirm completion or explain any necessary retention. Platform authorization can also be revoked in the account's application or business integration settings. Deleting application data does not automatically delete public videos; request deletion of those posts explicitly or remove them on the platform.

Children and contact

The videos may be intended for children, but this administrative application is for the adult account owner. It does not provide child accounts or ask children to submit personal information. Privacy questions can be sent to asifrezanit@gmail.com.
