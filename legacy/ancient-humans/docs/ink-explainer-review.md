## Observations and adaptation

12/18 titles contain the plural word Humans; 10/18 contain Ancient Humans. Three more use
singular Human. Animal recognition, time perception and IKEA effect use neither.
Use Ancient Humans consistently in this project's new titles as requested, rather than importing
these exceptions. Keyword inclusion describes the niche; it does not establish search volume or
causation. YouTube describes search using relevance, engagement and quality:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438?hl=en

The grid uses hand-drawn stick figures with large white round faces, black limbs, expressive poses,
flat colors and relatively detailed cartoon environments. Many later/history thumbnails use short
yellow captions with black outlines; others use white or black text. Scenes include sky/grassland,
caves, snowy shelters, deserts, mountains and white explanatory space. Caption positioning and
figures vary; do not use a single rigid composition for every topic.

Sampled interiors included an underground skeleton cross-section (day video around 0:38),
a running animal in grassland (day around 3:21), a labeled anatomical drawing on white (winter
around 3:27), a large text card (pleasure around 4:26), and a stick figure with spear in a sparse
landscape (terrifying-animal around 2:44). Sampling does not establish exact scene frequency,
full editing style, image-generation tools or the proportion of animation versus still images.
The retained system uses still images every seven seconds; it does not reproduce animated drawing.

The day video showed roughly 16M views, rain 1.8M, human species 1.5M and winter 1.3M at review.
These rounded, different-age public totals suggest useful reference themes but do not prove which
keywords or thumbnail details drove results. No promise of equivalent performance is made.
